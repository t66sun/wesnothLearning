## Training contrast

Each cell starts from the #306 qualified duel v38 BC actor and critic with an empty Adam state. Use the [existing BC, Astra and rules identities](../ppo_batch_comparison_20261107/inputs.json) and the [accepted Lite extension](../ppo_terminal_return_20261109/REPORT.md). The frozen BC opponent uses the same checkpoint's weights in greedy mode. Its export identity and the actual non-learner seat are recorded before play and checked on resume.

Both arms consume the accepted eight-game [ordinal stream](../ppo_batch_contract_20261107/driver.py) with equal seeds, board, ordered faction roster, learner seat and combat and learner-sampling seeds by ordinal. Baseline uses Astra in all eight opponent seats. Mixed assigns BC to the two zero-based positions in `bc_slots[batch_offset % 9]` and Astra to the other six. The nine-batch table gives BC nine games in each learner seat, three per learner faction and nine arena2 plus three on each native training board. Both seats are represented on each map and for each learner faction. Astra supplies all DAgger queries and the existing control-rehearsal exclusion. Only the learner side writes PPO rows. Both arms use `official-score-30-v1`, λ = 0.95, fixed shaping, historical-action CE, unchanged PPO/KL settings, eight game workers and one trainer thread.

Cells run serially with the order reversed in pair two. Each gets a 5,400 s hard cap and a common 5,100 s admission cutoff, reserving 300 s to settle admitted work. Select only the last complete PPO checkpoint settled by that cell's cutoff, without validation selection. Report the common prefix of paired training ordinals and the actual work of both arms. The overall clock includes up to 900 s preparation, 32,400 s PPO, 1,800 s Hamlets validation, 5,400 s holdout and 2,700 s audit, for 43,200 s. At launch require 30 GiB available on the run filesystem. The previous 840-game holdout finished in 2,043 s. The reservation allows for a slower board mix. ## Holdout and decision

Ur-Thorodor and Face of the Enemy are genuine Wesnoth 1.18.8 campaign layouts used with standard Lite duel initialization. Their frozen terrain and start positions match the rule catalog. Ur-Thorodor has ten villages; Face of the Enemy has zero, limiting its economics and the generality of the pooled estimate. The two layouts are independent of the audited initialization, qualification, training, validation and prior holdout layouts. Hamlets remains separate.

Use the existing ordered six-faction permutations and both learner seat rotations, with common combat seeds and Astra in the other seat. Compare shared BC and the six wall checkpoints. Score 60 per role per map, 840 games. Reserve one score per exact scenario/rotation per role even if weights coincide. Normalize official 3/1/0 points by three. Equal-weight maps after averaging their 30 scenario blocks. Run 10,000 crossed bootstrap draws using the frozen bootstrap seed, resampling the three paired training seeds and the scenarios within each map. Use score differences of mixed minus pure Astra and mixed minus shared BC. Report pooled, map and seed results, budget, fresh eligible decisions, committed updates, cases interrupted at the deadline, and actual opponent counts. Accept when the pooled differences reach +8 and +5 percentage points respectively and both paired 95% lower bounds exceed zero, with budget and data checks passing. Preserve and report the result regardless of outcome.
