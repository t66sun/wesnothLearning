Each seed uses the A2 common ordinal stream, with complete same-policy rollout groups and fixed Astra opponents.

Use the same 300 valid teacher-game requirement, common teacher scenario seed, original imitation/coverage/design gates, and 150-minute wall allowance. The exact historical actor initializer supplies the original base BC and both fresh BC runs. B and C apply their accepted weights-only transfers directly from that checkpoint, recording its provenance in the new run. The weights-only branch resets critic, Adam, corpus and gates for fresh BC. PPO pairs start from their own complete qualifying actor and critic with Adam reset. Report each arm's BC score, gate, coverage, games, and BC time separately.

The common task is `official-score-30-v1` with the original finite 30-round score, rules, opponent package, Lite teacher source, fixed reward, control exclusion, historical-action CE and KL 0.02. Keep the #227 learning rate, epochs, minibatch, entropy and rehearsal configuration. Use the same CPU host, eight game workers, one trainer thread, and pinned dependencies. The common inputs file records the exact BC, rule and Astra identities.

## Clock and checkpoints

Allocate 90 minutes per PPO cell including initialization. Admit new physical batches for the first 85 minutes and reserve the last five for settlement. The last fully settled checkpoint by the cell deadline is the primary endpoint. The first complete boundaries at or above 10,000 and 30,000 fresh eligible decisions are secondary endpoints. Neither threshold stops a batch or triggers extra time. A zero-step KL rejection consumes fresh targets and its unchanged weights can still mark a first crossing. Record exact thresholds, overshoots, absent endpoints, and unfinished work separately. Keep production validation and checkpoint selection, and report `selected` independently from `wall`. Preserve rollout, preparation, optimization, trainer RSS and complete-batch counters. Reuse the first and last settled trajectories for recorded-behavior MC diagnostics.

During the first A cell, read `budget.json` for `first_batch` and `history_start`. The matching schedule under `duel/schedules` identifies eight ordinals, one policy version and the eight planned games. The first new PPO history entry in `duel/state.json` and its job records show complete settlement and fresh targets. Compare this schedule with the first two completed four-game schedules for the same seed. The driver writes the paired `first-online-a8-<seed>.json` when both cells finish, before the remaining comparisons are required.

The full training allowance is 18 hours for twelve PPO cells and five hours for two fresh BC contracts. The launch manifest adds measured allowances for preflight, shared validation, shared holdout, reporting and any separately authorized final native comparison. Freeze the sum and the stage allocations once. Use one active CPU stage at a time. A stage may finish early. Its spare time never changes another cell's 90-minute cap. Record input preparation or transferred initializer work in the total ledger. Budget exhaustion and failed prerequisites end the affected planned comparison with its original evidence.

This design uses the physical terrain and its two starting positions with standard duel setup. The retained #227 optimization, validation and holdout boards and the prior formal run do not include this board. Conclusions cover Silverhead rather than map-to-map generalization. Use all 30 ordered nonmirror faction assignments, each with complete two-seat rotation and matched combat seeds. The former holdout maps remain historical reference.

A route qualifies for nomination if both its equal-wall change versus base and its increase over its own BC have positive lower 95% interval bounds. Choose the qualifying route with the highest base-relative point estimate and break exact ties in order A, B, C. Only that nominated route may enter the single final native comparison.
