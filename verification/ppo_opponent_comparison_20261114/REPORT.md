# Mixed-opponent integration

[#321](https://github.com/t66sun/wesnothLearning/issues/321) under [#319](https://github.com/t66sun/wesnothLearning/issues/319).

## Experiment entry point

`python -m verification.ppo_opponent_comparison_20261114.driver` prepares one immutable launch manifest and runs the authorized contrast by its SHA-256. The shared v38 BC tensor is copied to the experiment's greedy export. The training adapter verifies the tensor and mode before scheduling or resuming PPO. The fixed nine-batch table chooses the two BC opponent seats in the mixed arm. Validation, DAgger and the control exclusion use Astra. The established coordinator, learner-only row collection and PPO update handle both arms.

## Acceptance

Ubuntu used the accepted Lite extension, frozen 1.18.8 combat rules and Git-archived source. The scoped tests compare 27 consecutive baseline, mixed and legacy batches, replay a frozen schedule with no recollection, reject changed paths and config, load the actual greedy tensor, and exercise both learner seats with deterministic action fixtures. Only the learner side contributes written PPO and DAgger rows. Synthetic replies check the whole seven-role panel and the independent score calculation. The combined suite adds the finite-score and interrupted-update recovery checks.

An isolated reversion that removes the selected BC slots fails the quota assertion with two BC games expected and zero observed. A closed-admission multiprocess startup branches the qualifying BC checkpoint, verifies empty Adam, enters the mixed runner, and rejects any game request. No formal optimization or evaluation has run.

## Formal evidence

The driver records one job per role and rotation, even when checkpoints have identical weight digests. Deadline, actual work and both score contrasts determine the verdict. Raw JSON, schedules, result rows and logs are packed with a hash for the report. [Frozen protocol](../ppo_opponent_protocol_20261114/PROTOCOL.md)
