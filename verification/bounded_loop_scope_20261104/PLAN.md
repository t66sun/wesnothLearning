# Complete bounded loop on Ubuntu

## Failure analysis

The November 1 run stopped after 558.33 seconds when duel reached five BC batches. FFA and team had committed only one batch each. No PPO or delivery stage ran. The later ordinary v6 run established effective BC checkpoints in all three formats after 69m10s under its own 90-minute profile. The first 24 same-seed v6 teacher jobs covered 2,406/2,680 duel labels. The first 24 FFA jobs supplied only 25 held-out recruits. The ordinary gates require 90% coverage and 32 held-out examples per class. More optimizer work on these same rows cannot supply the missing labels. [Original bounded run](../bounded_loop_20261101/REPORT.md) · [Current teacher corpus and checkpoints](../bc_ordinary_v6_20261103/REPORT.md)

The old driver lets each requested PPO phase run until its deadline or hard batch cap. An optimizer deadline can leave pending work. This does not provide the intended successful stop at a committed boundary. For the final panel, 30 validation jobs in the archived v6 scheduler averaged 22.63, 49.55 and 74.07 worker-seconds per duel, FFA and team game. Applied to the required 60, 120 and 240 final games at full eight-worker occupancy, this projects about 52 minutes. That projection uses different scenarios and concurrent training. Measure the bottleneck with the frozen trained policy on a validation board before starting the timed acceptance.
